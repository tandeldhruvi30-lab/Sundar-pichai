About

Sundar Pichai is an Indian-American business executive and one of the most influential leaders in the technology industry. Born on 10 June 1972 in Madurai, Tamil Nadu, he grew up in Chennai in a modest middle-class family where access to technology was limited, which later shaped his belief in making technology available to everyone. After studying metallurgical engineering at IIT Kharagpur, he went on to Stanford University and the Wharton School in the United States. He worked at Applied Materials and McKinsey & Company before joining Google in 2004. At Google he led the Chrome browser project and later took charge of products such as Chrome OS, Google Drive and Android. He became CEO of Google in August 2015 and CEO of Alphabet Inc. in December 2019. Under his leadership, Google has adopted an AI-first approach, bringing artificial intelligence into Search, Assistant, Cloud and the Gemini models. He is known for his calm, humble and collaborative leadership style.

Major Contributions

  • Google Chrome

    Led the development and launch of the Chrome browser in 2008, now one of the most widely used browsers in the world.

  • Chrome OS

    Oversaw Chrome OS, the lightweight cloud-based operating system used in Chromebooks.

  • Android Leadership

    Took charge of Android in 2013, helping it grow into the world's most used mobile platform.

  • Google Drive & Apps

    Played a key role in products such as Google Drive and the wider suite of Google apps.

  • AI-First Strategy

    Pushed Google towards artificial intelligence across Search, Assistant, Photos, Cloud and the Gemini models.

  • Alphabet Leadership

    Guided Google and Alphabet as CEO, expanding Google Cloud and investing in long-term research projects.
